Step 1: Design and Prepress.

Prepare the artwork for printing by adjusting colors, layouts, and adding necessary guidelines or bleed areas.

Step 2: Preparing the Printing.

Send the design to a printer, which directly transfers the design onto the printing material using digital technology.

Step 3: Printing.

Start the printing process, where the ink is applied to the material either through direct contact or by digital printing technology.

Step 4: Inspection and Packaging.

Inspect & Package the printed items appropriately to protect them during transportation and storage.
